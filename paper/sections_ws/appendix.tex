\paragraph{Program repair.}
The same measurement on MBPP, where the observations are the Python
interpreter's own output. Actions here are whole functions, an order of
magnitude longer than tool calls, so raw nats are not comparable with
ToolShed; divided by action length they are, and both environments land at
close to one nat per action token.

scored candidate set; \emph{greedy} is the fraction of items on which greedy

\paragraph{Robustness.}
The assumption-light item-level sign statistic, the change in the
gold-minus-failed margin, and the decomposition referenced to the counterfactual
\emph{success} observation rather than the valence-free one. The two references
bracket any reasonable notion of neutrality.

\section{Cost}
\label{app:ws-cost}

Everything was produced on one laptop CPU (\CPUName, four physical cores,
17\,GB RAM) with no GPU, in float32. The probe is built out of teacher-forced
scoring rather than generation because on a CPU a single decoding step costs
about as much as thirty scored positions; prefix reuse across conditions
removes \CacheSaving\ of the token positions a naive implementation would push
through the network. Both recommended interventions are close to free: the
abstraction shortens the context and costs no extra model call, and the ban
adds one pass over a short list per decoding step and no generated tokens.
