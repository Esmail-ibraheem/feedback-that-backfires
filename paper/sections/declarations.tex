\section*{Data availability}

All code, the constructed probe items, the raw per-item log-probability records,
the free-running rollout transcripts and the per-run environment metadata are
publicly available at
\url{https://github.com/Esmail-ibraheem/feedback-that-backfires}. Every number,
table and figure in this paper is regenerated from those records by a documented
command sequence; none is transcribed by hand. The model checkpoints and the
MBPP dataset are public and are cited in the text.
